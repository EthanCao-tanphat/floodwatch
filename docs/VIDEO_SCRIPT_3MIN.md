# FloodWatch 3-Minute Video Script

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook

In Vietnam, millions of people depend on motorbikes every day. But during heavy rain, a route that looks normal can become risky very quickly because of poor drainage, tide pressure, or local flood hotspots.

Most map apps tell us the fastest route. FloodWatch asks a different question: which route is still passable for a motorbike in the next 30 to 60 minutes?

## 0:20–0:45 — Problem gap

Existing tools are often reactive. They show where water already is, or they provide general flood information. But riders need route-specific guidance before they enter a flooded street.

Our core idea is simple: UDI Maps tells riders where water already is; FloodWatch estimates where flood risk may hit their route next.

## 0:45–1:20 — What FloodWatch does

FloodWatch is a flood-aware routing app for Vietnamese motorbike riders. A rider enters an origin and destination, just like a normal map app.

The system then compares route options: the fastest route, the recommended route, and alternatives. But instead of ranking only by time, FloodWatch scores each route by predicted flood risk and motorbike passability.

## 1:20–2:00 — Demo walkthrough

Here, we enter a route from Huynh Tan Phat in District 7 to Vo Van Ngan in Thu Duc.

FloodWatch returns route options and shows the coverage tier. In Ho Chi Minh City, this is our strongest full prediction layer.

The selected route is divided into numbered segments. Each segment has a risk score and passability level. When we click a segment, the app explains the evidence: rainfall, tide pressure, hotspot proximity, drainage risk, and rider reports.

This matters because the fastest route is not always the safest route during flood risk.

## 2:00–2:30 — Rider report and evidence layer

FloodWatch also supports rider photo reports. A rider can upload a road photo, and the app classifies whether it looks safe, slow-pass, avoid for motorbikes, or impassable.

That report appears on the map and can increase risk for nearby route segments. This turns rider observations into live evidence for other users.

## 2:30–2:50 — Vietnam-wide expansion

FloodWatch is designed for Vietnam-wide routing, but we use honest confidence tiers. Ho Chi Minh City has the strongest evidence layer today. Other cities use partial or rainfall-first warnings until more verified local hotspot and drainage data is connected.

## 2:50–3:00 — Closing

FloodWatch turns flood information into route decisions. It helps motorbike riders avoid flood risk before it reaches their route.
